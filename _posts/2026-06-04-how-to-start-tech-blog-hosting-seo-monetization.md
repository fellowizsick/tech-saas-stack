49|## Step 2: Install WordPress and Choose a Theme
50|
51|Once your hosting is set up, most providers offer one-click WordPress installation through their control panel. Log into your hosting dashboard, find the WordPress installer (usually under "Auto Installer" or "Website" section), and follow the prompts.
52|
53|After WordPress is installed, pick a theme that prioritizes:
54|
55|- **Page speed** — Avoid bloated multipurpose themes. Lightweight options like GeneratePress, Astra, or Kadence load in under a second.
56|- **Typography** — Tech readers appreciate clean, readable fonts. Avoid anything trendy or hard to scan.
57|- **Layout flexibility** — You'll want a theme that supports full-width pages for comparison tables and review posts.
58|
59|For a tech blog, I recommend GeneratePress Premium ($59/year) or the free Astra theme. Both are optimized for Core Web Vitals out of the box.

197|## Step 7: Promote Your Content
198|
199|Writing great content is only half the battle. You need to get it in front of readers:
200|
201|1. **Pinterest** — Create pinnable images for your tutorials and comparisons. Pinterest drives significant traffic to blog content (and it's search-driven, not algorithmic).
202|2. **Hacker News** — If you write something genuinely insightful or data-driven, submit it to news.ycombinator.com. A front-page hit can drive 50,000+ visitors in a day.
203|3. **X/Twitter threads** — Turn your post into a 10-tweet thread with a link back to the full article.
204|4. **Reddit** — Participate genuinely in relevant subreddits (r/webhosting, r/WordPress, r/Blogging). Don't spam — add value first.
205|5. **Email list** — Send every new post to your subscribers. Aim for one high-quality email per week.
