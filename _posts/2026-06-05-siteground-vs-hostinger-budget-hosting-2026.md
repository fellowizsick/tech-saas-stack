<summary>Pricing & Value</summary>

### SiteGround Pricing

SiteGround offers three shared hosting tiers:

- **StartUp** — $2.99/mo for 3 years (renews at ~$17.99/mo): 10 GB storage, ~10,000 monthly visits
- **GrowBig** — $4.99/mo for 3 years (renews at ~$24.99/mo): 20 GB storage, ~100,000 visits, plus staging and on-demand backups
- **GoGeek** — $7.99/mo for 3 years (renews at ~$39.99/mo): 40 GB storage, ~400,000 visits, white-label hosting, and Git integration

The key thing to understand about SiteGround is the **renewal price jump**. The intro rates are aggressively low, but after the initial term, prices triple. This is the biggest complaint you'll see in reviews — and it's worth factoring into your long-term budget.

### Hostinger Pricing

Hostinger's shared hosting (they call it Web Hosting) plans:

- **Premium** — $2.69/mo for 4 years (renews at ~$7.99/mo): 100 GB, ~25,000 visits, free domain
- **Business** — $3.99/mo for 4 years (renews at ~$8.99/mo): 200 GB NVMe, ~100,000 visits, daily backups, free domain
- **Cloud Startup** — $8.99/mo for 4 years (renews at ~$19.99/mo): 200 GB, ~300,000 visits, dedicated resources

Hostinger's renewal prices are much gentler — roughly 2-3x the intro rate vs SiteGround's 5-6x jump. If you're planning to keep your site live for more than a year or two, Hostinger's long-term cost is significantly lower.

**Winner: Hostinger** — Better intro pricing AND lower renewals.

<details class="collapsible-section" markdown="1">
<summary>Performance & Speed</summary>

Hosting performance affects everything from user experience to SEO rankings. Let's look at how these two stack up.

### SiteGround Performance

SiteGround has invested heavily in speed infrastructure over the past few years:

- **Ultrafast PHP** powered by custom-developed speed optimizations
- **NGINX-based caching** with dynamic cache, static cache, and memcached
- **Free Cloudflare CDN** integrated at the server level
- **NVMe SSD storage** on all plans
- **HTTP/3 and QUIC support** for faster connections

SiteGround's response times consistently clock in around 400-600ms in independent tests — excellent for shared hosting.

### Hostinger Performance

Hostinger also delivers strong performance metrics:

- **LiteSpeed web server** with built-in caching (faster than Apache for WordPress)
- **LiteSpeed Cache for WordPress** plugin pre-installed
- **NVMe SSD storage** (up to 200 GB on Business plan)
- **Free Cloudflare CDN**
- **8 global data centers** to serve visitors from their nearest location

Hostinger tends to edge out SiteGround slightly on raw speed, with load times averaging 350-500ms in benchmarks, thanks to LiteSpeed's more efficient caching engine.

**Winner: Tie** — Both deliver excellent performance for budget hosting. Hostinger has a slight edge on raw speed; SiteGround's infrastructure is more battle-tested.

<summary>Features & Ease of Use</summary>

### SiteGround's Approach

SiteGround uses a **custom control panel** built on top of cPanel technology. It's clean, intuitive, and well-organized — much less overwhelming than traditional cPanel. Highlights:

- **Site Tools dashboard** with clear navigation for files, databases, email, and domains
- **1-click WordPress installer** with automatic setup optimization
- **Staging environment** on all plans (GrowBig+)
- **SG Optimizer plugin** for WordPress caching and performance
- **Collaboration tools** (add users, set permissions)
- **Smart AI anti-bot system** for login security

### Hostinger's Approach

Hostinger uses **hPanel**, their proprietary control panel. It's even simpler than SiteGround's and feels more modern:

- **hPanel dashboard** — minimalist, task-oriented interface
- **WordPress on-boarding wizard** that pre-installs themes, plugins, and SSL
- **1-click staging** on Premium+ plans
- **LiteSpeed Cache** pre-installed and auto-configured
- **AI website builder** included (if you want a drag-and-drop option)
- **Auto-updates** for WordPress core, themes, and plugins

Both control panels are beginner-friendly, but Hostinger's setup wizard makes the first-time experience smoother — especially if you're not technical.

**Winner: Hostinger** (slightly) — Better onboarding, simpler panel, AI site builder as a bonus.

<summary>FAQ</summary>

### Is Hostinger better than SiteGround?
For most users, Hostinger offers better value — lower prices, more storage, and faster LiteSpeed servers. SiteGround is better if you prioritize premium customer support.

### Does SiteGround include free backups?
Yes, daily backups are included on all SiteGround plans with 30-day retention. GrowBig and GoGeek plans also offer on-demand backups.

### Is Hostinger good for WordPress?
Absolutely. Hostinger's LiteSpeed caching + auto-optimized WordPress setup delivers excellent performance. The Business plan includes managed WordPress features like staging and auto-updates.

### Which host is cheaper long-term?
Hostinger is significantly cheaper at renewal. SiteGround's intro rates are competitive, but renewal prices jump to $18-40/mo. Hostinger renews at $8-20/mo.

### Do both hosts offer a money-back guarantee?
Yes — both SiteGround and Hostinger offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all shared hosting plans.

### Can I host multiple sites on SiteGround?
SiteGround limits you to 1 site on StartUp, unlimited on GrowBig (but with storage constraints). Hostinger's Business plan allows up to 100 websites.

### Which is better for eCommerce?
Both work well with WooCommerce. Hostinger's Business plan offers more storage for product images, while SiteGround's GoGeek plan provides better performance for high-traffic stores.
