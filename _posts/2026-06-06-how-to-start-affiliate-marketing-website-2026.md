<summary>Step 1: Choose Your Niche</summary>

Your niche determines everything — your content topics, the affiliate programs you join, your audience, and your earning potential. A good niche has three qualities:

- **You have genuine interest or expertise** — you'll write dozens of posts, so don't pick something you'll abandon in a month
- **Products exist with affiliate programs** — the SaaS and hosting stack we cover on this blog is an example of a niche with high-value commissions
- **Real search volume** — people must be actively searching for information on the topic

| Niche | Commission Range | Competition | Example Affiliates |

The most sustainable approach is picking a **sub-niche** within a broad category. Instead of "web hosting," focus on "managed WordPress hosting for e-commerce." Instead of "SEO tools," focus on "local SEO tools for small businesses."

<summary>Step 3: Set Up WordPress</summary>

Once your hosting is ready, install WordPress. Most hosts offer one-click installation:

1. **Log into your hosting control panel** (cPanel, hPanel, or Site Tools)
2. **Find the WordPress installer** — it's usually called "Auto Installer" or "WordPress"
3. **Enter your site details** — site name, admin username, and password
4. **Pick a theme** — for affiliate marketing, choose a lightweight, SEO-friendly theme like GeneratePress, Astra, or Kadence

For a full walkthrough, check our guide on <a href="/tech-saas-stack/2026/06/how-to-set-up-wordpress-site-hostinger-guide/">How to Set Up a WordPress Site on Hostinger</a>.

### Content Formats That Convert

Affiliate content isn't one-size-fits-all. The best affiliate sites use a mix of these formats:

| Format | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **How-to guides** | Capture informational traffic at the top of the funnel | "How to start a blog in 2026" |
| **Comparison posts** | Target people deciding between products | "Hostinger vs SiteGround" |
| **Reviews** | Capture readers with high purchase intent | "WP Engine Review 2026" |
| **Roundups** | Broader keyword reach, multiple affiliate opportunities | "10 Best Web Hosting Providers" |
| **Problem/solution** | Solve a specific pain point | "How to Speed Up WordPress" |

### SEO Best Practices for Affiliate Content

- **Target keyword in your title, H1, and first 100 words**
- **Write 2,000+ words** — long-form content consistently outranks short content
- **Use H2 and H3 subheadings** with secondary keywords
- **Add internal links** to 2–3 other posts on your blog
- **Include a table of contents** — it earns featured snippets
- **Add FAQ schema** for rich results
- **Optimize images** — compress them and use descriptive alt text

<details class="collapsible-section" markdown="1">
<summary>Step 7: Drive Traffic</summary>

Content alone won't bring visitors. You need a traffic strategy:

### Organic Search (The Long Game)

SEO takes 3–6 months to show results, but it's the most sustainable traffic source. Focus on:
- Publishing 2–4 articles per week
- Building backlinks through guest posts and collaborations
- Updating old content to keep it fresh
- Fixing technical SEO issues (site speed, mobile optimization, Core Web Vitals)

### Social Media (The Accelerator)

Share every new post on:
- **Twitter/X** — engage in niche conversations, share insights
- **LinkedIn** — great for B2B and SaaS affiliate niches
- **Pinterest** — excellent for visual niches (design, home office, recipes)
- **Reddit** — participate in relevant subreddits (but read the rules first — self-promotion gets banned fast)

### Email List (The Money Maker)

An email list converts 3–5x better than any other channel. Start collecting emails from day one with a free lead magnet (checklist, template, cheat sheet). Tools like <a href="https://activecampaign.com/">ActiveCampaign</a> make it easy to set up automated email sequences that send your best affiliate content to new subscribers.

</details>

<details class="collapsible-section" markdown="1">
<summary>Step 8: Optimize and Scale</summary>

Once you have 20–30 published posts and some traffic, it's time to optimize:

1. **Track everything** — Set up Google Analytics and Google Search Console from day one
2. **Identify your top performers** — Find which articles drive the most traffic and conversions
3. **Update and expand** — Add new sections, update prices, and improve CTAs on your best posts
4. **Build topical authority** — Create clusters of content around one topic (pillar page + supporting articles)
5. **Diversify income streams** — Add display ads, digital products, or a membership tier on top of affiliate income

<summary>Frequently Asked Questions</summary>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question"><strong>How much money can I make with an affiliate marketing website?</strong></div>
<div class="faq-answer">Income varies wildly. Beginners with consistent effort (20+ quality articles) typically earn $200–$1,000/month within 6–12 months. Top sites in competitive niches can earn $10K–$50K+/month. The key is choosing high-commission programs — a single WP Engine sale ($200+) equals 40 Hostinger sales at $5 each.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question"><strong>Do I need to disclose affiliate links?</strong></div>
<div class="faq-answer">Yes. The FTC requires clear and conspicuous disclosure of any affiliate relationships. Place a disclosure at the top of every post (like the one at the top of this article) and near any affiliate links. Failing to disclose can result in fines and legal action.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question"><strong>How long does it take to start making money?</strong></div>
<div class="faq-answer">Most new affiliate sites see their first commission in 3–6 months. SEO is a slow burn — Google takes time to trust new sites. Use social media and forums to drive early traffic while your SEO builds momentum.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question"><strong>Can I start an affiliate site without any money?</strong></div>
<div class="faq-answer">Technically yes (free platforms like WordPress.com or Medium), but you'll face severe limitations — no custom domain, no monetization control, and limited SEO features. We recommend investing at least $30–$50 for a domain and a month of hosting. Hostinger at $2.99/mo is the cheapest way to start professionally.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question"><strong>How many articles do I need to publish before I see traffic?</strong></div>
<div class="faq-answer">There's no magic number, but 30–50 well-optimized articles is a realistic target for meaningful organic traffic. Most sites hit a growth inflection point around 30 articles. Consistency matters more than volume — one good article per week beats ten rushed ones.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question"><strong>What's the difference between affiliate marketing and dropshipping?</strong></div>
<div class="faq-answer">In affiliate marketing, you earn a commission for referring sales — you never handle inventory, shipping, or customer service. In dropshipping, you run the store and handle orders, but a third party fulfills them. Affiliate marketing has lower risk, lower overhead, and simpler operations, making it the better choice for content creators and bloggers.</div>
