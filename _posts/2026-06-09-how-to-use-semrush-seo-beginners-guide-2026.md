<summary>Getting Started: Domain Analytics</summary>


Domain Analytics is the first tool every Semrush beginner should open. It gives you an immediate snapshot of any website's SEO health.

### How to Run Your First Domain Analysis

1. **Log in** to your Semrush account and click **Domain Analytics** in the left sidebar
2. **Enter your domain** (e.g., `yoursite.com`) and click **Search**
3. Review the **Overview** dashboard showing:
   - **Authority Score** — a composite metric (1-100) predicting search ranking ability
   - **Organic Search Traffic** — estimated monthly visits from organic search
   - **Top Keywords** — queries driving the most traffic to your domain
   - **Backlinks** — total referring domains and links pointing to your site
   - **Keyword Positions** — distribution across top 3, top 10, and top 100 rankings

For example, entering a competitor's domain reveals their top-ranking keywords at a glance. This is the fastest way to identify content gaps and opportunities in your niche.

### Using Domain vs URL Analysis

Semrush distinguishes between **Domain Analytics** (the entire website's SEO profile) and **URL Analytics** (a single page's performance). Use Domain Analytics for competitive benchmarking; use URL Analytics to diagnose why a specific page is underperforming.

<summary>Keyword Research: Finding Your Traffic Goldmine</summary>


Semrush's keyword research tools are arguably its strongest feature. The platform combines search volume data, keyword difficulty scores, and SERP features to help you pick the right targets.

### Keyword Overview

The **Keyword Overview** tool gives you everything you need to evaluate a keyword opportunity:

- **Volume** — Average monthly searches (desktop + mobile)
- **Keyword Difficulty (KD%)** — 0-100% score; higher = harder to rank
- **Global Volume** — Searches across all regions
- **Trend** — Seasonality and 12-month trend line
- **SERP Features** — Featured snippets, People Also Ask, image packs, video carousels
- **CPC** — Cost-per-click for PPC campaigns

### Keyword Magic Tool

The **Keyword Magic Tool** is Semrush's primary keyword research engine. Enter a seed keyword (e.g., "managed WordPress hosting") and it returns **millions of related keywords** organized by match type:

- **Broad Match** — All keywords containing any term from your seed
- **Phrase Match** — Keywords containing your exact phrase
- **Exact Match** — Keywords exactly matching your seed phrase
- **Related** — Semantically related keywords without your seed terms

Each keyword shows volume, KD%, trend, SERP features, and CPC. Export the list as CSV or send high-priority keywords directly to your Position Tracking project.

For a practical walkthrough of applying keyword research to actual content, our [How to Run an SEO Audit with Ahrefs](/tech-saas-stack/2026/06/06/how-to-run-seo-audit-ahrefs-guide/) guide covers a similar workflow that works with any SEO tool.

### Keyword Gap Analysis

The **Keyword Gap** tool compares your domain against up to 4 competitors to identify:

- **Shared keywords** — Rankings both you and competitors hold
- **Missing keywords** — Keywords competitors rank for but you don't
- **Untapped keywords** — Keywords nobody in your set ranks for
- **Strongest keywords** — Keywords where you outperform all competitors

This is the single most valuable competitive research exercise you can do. Running a Keyword Gap analysis once a month ensures you never miss an emerging content opportunity.

<summary>Site Audit: Fix Technical SEO Issues</summary>


A beautiful site with thin content won't rank — and a technically broken site won't rank either. Semrush's **Site Audit** tool crawls your entire website and produces a report of every technical issue affecting your SEO.

### Setting Up Your First Audit

1. From the dashboard, go to **Projects** → **Create Project** → **Site Audit**
2. Configure crawl settings (max pages, crawl speed, user agent)
3. Set the target audience (Googlebot, Googlebot Smartphone, Bingbot, etc.)
4. Run the audit — it typically completes in 2-10 minutes depending on site size

The report organizes issues into these categories:

- **Errors** — Critical problems (404 pages, broken redirects, missing meta tags)
- **Warnings** — Moderate issues (duplicate content, slow pages, oversized images)
- **Notices** — Minor optimization opportunities (low word count, thin content)

Each issue includes a detailed explanation, affected URLs, and remediation steps. Many issues link directly to Semrush Academy lessons explaining how to fix them.

### Crawlability and Indexability

The **Crawlability** section tells search engines which pages to index and which to skip. Key checks include:

- Robots.txt configuration and errors
- XML sitemap validation
- Internal link structure (orphan pages, redirect chains)
- HTTPS vs HTTP status codes
- Canonical URL issues

<details class="collapsible-section" markdown="1">
<summary>Position Tracking: Monitor Your Rankings</summary>


Once you've optimized your site and created content targeting specific keywords, you need to track whether those efforts are paying off. Semrush's **Position Tracking** tool handles this.

### Creating a Position Tracking Project

1. Go to **Projects** → **Create Project** → **Position Tracking**
2. Enter your domain and target search engine (Google, Bing, Yahoo, Yandex)
3. Choose locations (country, city, or ZIP-level for local SEO)
4. Add up to 500 keywords (Pro plan) — import from Keyword Magic Tool or paste manually
5. Choose device (desktop, mobile, tablet, or all)

The tool then checks your rankings **daily** and reports:

- **Average Position** — Overall ranking health across all tracked keywords
- **Visibility** — Percentage of potential clicks based on your positions
- **Estimated Traffic** — Projected monthly visits from ranked keywords
- **SERP Features** — Featured snippets, People Also Ask, and other rich results
- **Distribution** — Chart of keywords in positions 1-3, 4-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51-100

### Competitor Rank Tracking

You can add up to 20 competitors to any Position Tracking project. The tool then shows who's winning for each keyword and how your position changes relative to competitors over time.

<summary>Backlink Analysis: Build Authority</summary>


Backlinks remain one of Google's top three ranking signals in 2026. Semrush's **Backlink Analytics** tools help you build and maintain a healthy backlink profile.

### Backlink Analytics Overview

Enter any domain to see:

- **Total Backlinks** and **Referring Domains**
- **Authority Score** of the referring domain
- **Follow vs Nofollow** ratio
- **Anchor Text Distribution** — keyword-rich vs branded vs generic anchors
- **Top Referring Domains** — highest-authority sites linking to you
- **Indexed Pages** — pages from that domain in Semrush's index

### Backlink Gap Analysis

Similar to Keyword Gap, **Backlink Gap** compares your backlink profile against up to 5 competitors and identifies:

- **Shared backlinks** — Sites linking to you and competitors
- **Competitor-only backlinks** — Link opportunities you're missing
- **Your unique backlinks** — Sites that only link to you (your competitive advantage)

Export competitor-only backlinks as a link-building prospecting list. Run this monthly to keep your pipeline full.

### Backlink Audit Tool

Not all backlinks are good. Toxic links from spammy sites can trigger Google penalties. Semrush's **Backlink Audit Tool** scores each link as toxic or clean and generates a **disavow file** (.txt) you can submit to Google's Disavow Tool. Run this quarterly to protect your site from manual actions.

<summary>Frequently Asked Questions</summary>


### Is Semrush worth the price for a single blog?

Yes, if you're treating your blog as a serious business. The Pro plan at $129.95/month pays for itself if even one piece of keyword-optimized content brings in additional affiliate revenue or product sales. For hobby blogs, the free tier (10 daily searches) may be sufficient initially.

### Does Semrush offer a free version?

Yes, Semrush has a limited free account that allows up to 10 searches per day across Keyword Overview, Domain Analytics, and other basic tools. It's useful for testing the platform, but serious SEO work requires a paid plan.

### Can I use Semrush for YouTube SEO?

Yes, Semrush includes YouTube-specific keyword research through its Keyword Magic Tool (select YouTube as the search engine). You can analyze video keywords, track YouTube ranking positions, and perform channel-level competitor analysis.

### How accurate is Semrush's traffic data?

Semrush's traffic estimates are based on a mix of panel data, ISP data, and statistical modeling. For popular sites with significant organic traffic, estimates are typically within ±20% of Google Analytics data. For smaller or niche sites, accuracy decreases because the panel has fewer data points. Always use estimates as directional benchmarks, not precise analytics.

### Does Semrush integrate with Google Search Console?

Yes, Semrush connects directly to Google Search Console. Once connected, you can import your top 1,000 Google Search Console queries, analyze click-through rates, and identify pages losing impressions or clicks — all within Semrush's dashboard.
