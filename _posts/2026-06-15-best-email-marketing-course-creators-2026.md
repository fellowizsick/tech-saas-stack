<summary>Deep Dives</summary>


### 1. ActiveCampaign — The Power User's Choice

**Pricing (2026, annual billing):**
- **Lite**: $29/mo — 1,000 contacts, basic automation, no CRM
- **Plus**: $49/mo — 1,000 contacts, CRM, landing pages, SMS
- **Professional**: $149/mo — 1,000 contacts, predictive sending, attribution
- **Enterprise**: $145/mo+ — Custom, dedicated support, custom objects

**Why course creators choose it:**
ActiveCampaign's automation builder is unmatched. You can build complex conditional flows: "If contact clicked link X but didn't purchase in 3 days, send email Y, then if they open but don't click, add to SMS sequence, then if still nothing, notify sales team via Slack." That level of granularity recovers sales.

The **native CRM** is the killer feature for course businesses selling high-ticket ($500+). You can track deal stages, assign tasks, score leads based on email engagement + website behavior + custom fields. No Zapier duct tape needed.

**Downsides:**
- Steep learning curve. Expect 2-4 weeks to get competent.
- No native course hosting. You'll integrate with Teachable, Kajabi, or Thinkific.
- Pricing jumps hard at Professional tier. Most course creators need Plus ($49) or Professional ($149).
- Lite plan ($29) is too limited for serious automation — no CRM, no split automation.

**My take:** If you're doing $10k+/month in course revenue and need sophisticated funnels, ActiveCampaign pays for itself in recovered carts alone. If you're under $5k/mo, it's overkill.

---

### 2. ConvertKit (now Kit) — The Creator Favorite

**Pricing (2026, annual billing):**
- **Newsletter (Free)**: 1,000 subscribers, 1 basic visual automation, unlimited broadcasts, landing pages, forms
- **Creator**: $33/mo ($390/yr) — 1,000 subs, unlimited automations, sequences, Kit MCP, A/B testing, SMS, remove branding
- **Pro**: $66/mo ($790/yr) — 1,000 subs, Subscriber Signals, engagement analytics, unlimited users, deliverability reporting, referral system

**Why course creators choose it:**
Built by Nathan Barry specifically for creators. The UX shows it — visual automations feel like drawing a flowchart, not configuring software. **Kit Commerce** (digital product selling) is now native: you can sell courses, subscriptions, coaching, and downloads directly in Kit with 3.5% + 30¢ transaction fees (Free/Creator) or 0% on Pro.

The **Creator Network** lets you recommend other creators' newsletters and get recommended back — organic growth engine unique to Kit.

**Downsides:**
- No CRM. Lead management is tag-based only.
- No native course *hosting* — Kit Commerce delivers digital files, but no video hosting, progress tracking, or student dashboard. You still need a course platform for real courses.
- SMS is US/Canada only and costs extra credits.
- Pricing scales per 1,000 subs. At 10k subs, Creator is $83/mo, Pro is $141/mo.

**My take:** Best starting point for solo creators earning $0-$10k/mo. The free plan is genuinely usable. Migration path to ActiveCampaign later is clean if you outgrow it.

---

### 3. MailerLite — The Budget Powerhouse

**Pricing (2026, annual billing):**
- **Free**: 500 subscribers, 12,000 emails/mo, 1 website, 10 landing pages, 1 digital product, email automation builder
- **Growing Business**: $9/mo ($108/yr) — 500 subs, unlimited emails, 3 seats, unlimited websites/landing pages, 3 digital products, promotion pop-ups, multivariate testing
- **Advanced**: $18/mo ($216/yr) — 500 subs, unlimited everything, 5 seats, custom user permissions, dedicated IP option, deliverability consultation

**Why course creators choose it:**
Insane value. **$9/month for unlimited automations, unlimited landing pages, websites, and digital product sales** (with Stripe integration, 0% MailerLite fee on paid plans). The drag-drop editor is the best in class for non-technical users.

Recently added **AI writing assistant**, **smart sending** (optimize send times), and **Facebook custom audiences** sync on paid plans.

**Downsides:**
- No CRM, no lead scoring.
- Automation builder is simpler — no complex branching logic like ActiveCampaign.
- Deliverability good but not enterprise-grade.
- Free plan caps at 500 subs and 12k emails/mo (most will hit email limit first).
- Digital products feature is basic — file delivery only, no course structure, progress tracking, or community features.

**My take:** No-brainer for beginners under $3k/mo course revenue. You get 90% of what you need for 10% of ActiveCampaign's price. Graduate to ActiveCampaign when you need CRM or complex conditional logic.

---

### 4. GetResponse — The All-in-One Contender

**Pricing (2026, annual billing, 1,000 contacts):**
- **Starter**: $15.58/mo — Email marketing, basic automation, landing pages, forms
- **Marketer**: $48.38/mo — Full automation, webinars (100 attendees), funnels, ecommerce integrations
- **Creator**: $56.58/mo — Everything in Marketer + course builder, paid newsletters, premium webinars (300 attendees), webinar funnels
- **Enterprise**: Custom

**Why course creators choose it:**
**Creator plan is a true all-in-one:** email + automation + webinars + funnels + course builder + paid newsletters in one subscription. No duct-taping Teachable + ActiveCampaign + Zoom + ClickFunnels.

The **course builder** includes video hosting, drip content, quizzes, certificates, and student progress tracking. Webinar funnels (registration → reminder → replay → offer) are native and conversion-optimized.

**Downsides:**
- Course builder is functional but not as polished as Kajabi/Teachable.
- Email automation UI feels dated compared to Kit/ActiveCampaign.
- Pricing is per-contact-tier; jumps at 2,500, 5,000, 10,000 contacts.
- Deliverability historically good but had issues in 2023-24 (reported on Reddit/G2).
- 30-day trial only, no permanent free plan.

**My take:** Compelling if you want to consolidate tools and save on Kajabi ($149/mo) + ActiveCampaign ($149/mo) + Zoom ($15/mo). At $56.58/mo Creator plan, you replace 3-4 tools. Risk: jack of all trades, master of none.

<summary>FAQ</summary>


**Q: Can I sell courses directly in ConvertKit without Teachable?**
A: Kit Commerce delivers digital files (PDFs, videos, audio) and handles subscriptions/coaching payments. But no video hosting, progress tracking, quizzes, or student dashboard. For a real course experience, you still need a course platform. Kit works for simple digital products and coaching.

**Q: Is ActiveCampaign worth 5x the price of MailerLite?**
A: If you need CRM, lead scoring, complex conditional automation, or sales team integration — yes. If you just send broadcasts and basic sequences — no. Most course creators under $10k/mo don't need ActiveCampaign yet.

**Q: Does GetResponse's course builder replace Kajabi?**
A: Functionally, yes — video hosting, drip, quizzes, certificates, progress tracking. Polish-wise, no. Kajabi's student experience is smoother. GetResponse saves you $149/mo but you trade some UX. Test the 30-day trial with a real course before committing.

**Q: Which has the best deliverability in 2026?**
A: ActiveCampaign and ConvertKit consistently top independent deliverability tests. MailerLite and GetResponse are good but occasionally flagged for shared IP reputation issues on lower tiers. For high-volume senders (50k+ subs), dedicated IP options matter — ActiveCampaign Enterprise and GetResponse Enterprise offer them.

**Q: Can I migrate later without losing automations?**
A: Mostly yes. Tags, custom fields, and basic sequences export/import clean. Complex conditional logic (ActiveCampaign's "if/else/wait" branches) doesn't map 1:1 to simpler builders. Plan your migration: export automations as documentation, rebuild in new platform, run parallel for 30 days.
