## What Agencies Need from a WordPress Host — Different from Everyone Else

If you're managing even three client sites, your hosting needs shift dramatically from a single-site owner. Here's what matters when you're in the agency seat:

**Multi-site management dashboards.** Logging into separate cPanels or accounts for each client is a nightmare. You need a single dashboard where you can see all your environments, deploy updates, and check performance.

**Staging environments per project.** Every client sends changes — "can we test this plugin?" — and staging keeps those experiments off the live site. For agencies, staging isn't a nice-to-have, it's a daily workflow requirement.

**Team access controls.** You need to give developers SFTP access, give clients monitoring-only view, and keep full admin for yourself — without sharing root passwords.

**Client billing separation.** If each client gets their own server (or at least their own site quota), billing should be clear and itemized. No mystery charges.

**Migration tooling.** Moving sites from a client's old host is often the first thing you do. A good migration tool saves your onboarding team hours per client.

The providers below handle these differently. Let's dig into each one.

## How to Choose the Right Agency Hosting Setup

Every agency's hosting needs evolve. Here's a roadmap based on where you are:

### Solo Freelancer (1-5 client sites)

**Start with SiteGround GoGeek** ($7.99/mo intro, $39.99/mo renewal). You get unlimited sites, staging, white-label client area, and priority support. One dashboard for everything. When you hit 5+ sites, evaluate whether the renewal price or shared resources become limiting.

### Growing Agency (5-15 client sites)

**Mix Cloudways + SiteGround.** Move your higher-traffic clients to Cloudways (each on their own server at $14-28/mo) for performance isolation. Keep smaller client sites on SiteGround for simplicity. You'll have two dashboards to monitor, but your clients get the right level of service for their budget.

### Scaling Agency (15+ client sites)

**Go all-in on Cloudways.** Each client (or client group) gets their own server stack with team access control. Use the pay-as-you-go model to match billing to client counts. Add ScalaHosting's cloud VPS as a budget tier for smaller clients who still need dedicated resources.

### Enterprise Agency (high-budget clients)

**Use WP Engine** for your top-tier clients. The Genesis framework, Studio Sync, and EverCache justify the premium pricing when your clients are paying $500+/mo for site management.

## FAQ

### Can I manage all my client sites from one dashboard?

Yes — but it depends on the host. SiteGround shows all your sites in one Site Tools interface. Cloudways shows all your servers (each potentially hosting multiple client sites) in one dashboard. ScalaHosting SPanel gives you reseller-style multi-account management. WP Engine requires separate accounts per plan.

### Do I need a separate server per client?

Not necessarily, but it helps. Cloudways lets you run multiple sites on one server (using Nginx reverse proxy or separate application containers), and you can split them across servers as they grow. SiteGround containers isolate each site on the same server. The key metric is: does Client A's traffic spike affect Client B? If yes, it's time for separation.

### What's the most cost-effective setup for a new agency?

SiteGround GoGeek ($7.99/mo intro). You get unlimited sites, staging, and white-label support for less than the cost of a single VPS elsewhere. As you grow past 10 sites, add Cloudways servers for your top clients and keep smaller ones on SiteGround.

### Is white-label hosting important for agency client retention?

Yes — if clients see your dashboard, they'll remember they could cut you out and go direct. SiteGround's GoGeek white-label and ScalaHosting's SPanel reseller accounts prevent this. Cloudways doesn't offer white-label, so your clients see the Cloudways interface, which means they could theoretically move their server to their own account.

### How do staging environments work when managing multiple clients?

**Cloudways:** One-click staging on every server. Clone your live site to staging, make changes, push back to live. **SiteGround:** Staging copies each site to an isolated environment — test plugin updates, theme changes, content edits. **ScalaHosting:** SPanel staging mirrors the live environment. **WP Engine:** Staging is included but requires a full copy of the site, which takes a few minutes for larger sites.
