## FAQ — Email Marketing Platforms

### What is the cheapest email marketing platform in 2026?

MailerLite's free plan and Kit's free plan are tied for the cheapest option. Both support 1,000 subscribers at no cost. MailerLite gives you 12,000 emails per month; Kit offers unlimited emails. For paid plans, MailerLite's Growing Business plan at $11/mo is the most affordable entry point for 1,000 contacts.

### Which email platform has the best automation?

ActiveCampaign has the most powerful automation engine among the platforms I tested. Its visual builder supports conditional splits, goal-based triggers, and multi-step sequences with lead scoring. GetResponse is a close second with unlimited automation workflows on its Marketer plan.

### Is Mailchimp still good in 2026?

Yes, Mailchimp remains a strong option for ecommerce businesses. Its Shopify and WooCommerce integrations are deeper than any competitor, and the AI content optimizer is genuinely helpful. However, the pricing model (paying per total contacts rather than active subscribers) makes it more expensive for list sizes above 5,000 compared to competitors like MailerLite and Kit.

### Do I need a web hosting plan for email marketing?

You can use any email marketing platform without hosting, but if you're sending email campaigns with links to a website, your hosting provider affects deliverability and page load speed. For a high-performance WordPress site that integrates with ActiveCampaign or Mailchimp, I recommend <a href="https://www.cloudways.com/en/?id=2179745" rel="nofollow sponsored" target="_blank">Cloudways managed cloud hosting</a> or <a href="https://siteground.com/go/affiliate" rel="nofollow sponsored" target="_blank">SiteGround WordPress hosting</a> — both offer excellent speed and one-click installs for email marketing integrations.

### Can I switch email platforms later?

Yes, all five platforms support list import and export via CSV. ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, and GetResponse offer dedicated migration tools that transfer your templates, automations, and subscriber data with minimal downtime. Kit and MailerLite provide guided migration support for free accounts. Plan for 24-48 hours of transition time and notify your subscribers about any delivery delays.
