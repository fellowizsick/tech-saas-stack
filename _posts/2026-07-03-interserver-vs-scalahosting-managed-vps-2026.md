## Control Panel: cPanel vs SPanel

One of the biggest practical differences between these two hosts is the control panel you'll use every day.

**InterServer uses standard cPanel**, which is the industry-standard control panel. If you've hosted anywhere before, you already know how to use it. The downside is that cPanel licensing costs money (InterServer absorbs this in your plan price), and cPanel has been criticized for being somewhat dated in its interface compared to newer panels.

**ScalaHosting uses SPanel**, its proprietary in-house control panel developed specifically to replace cPanel. SPanel is included free with all ScalaHosting plans — no licensing fees. It offers:

- A modern, clean interface that's easier to navigate than cPanel
- Built-in security features (SPanel includes a web application firewall and malware scanner)
- One-click WordPress installer and management
- Built-in email management
- Free SSL management

For most users, SPanel is a genuine improvement over cPanel. It's faster, more intuitive, and doesn't add licensing overhead. If you're comfortable with cPanel, SPanel takes about an afternoon to learn — the concepts are the same, just organized differently.

## Performance and Infrastructure

**InterServer** runs on standard SSD storage with LiteSpeed caching on its shared plans. The company owns its own hardware and data centers, which gives it direct control over performance tuning. Most shared hosting users find InterServer's speed adequate for standard WordPress sites — it's not the fastest shared host, but it's reliably consistent.

**ScalaHosting** uses cloud-based infrastructure running on its own SPanel Cloud Platform. All VPS plans use NVMe SSD storage (faster than standard SSDs), and resources are dedicated rather than shared. This means your site's performance isn't affected by noisy neighbors — the $29.95/mo plan gives you 2 dedicated vCPU cores and 4 GB of RAM that are yours alone.

In real-world terms: if you're running a single WordPress blog with a few thousand monthly visitors, InterServer's shared hosting handles it without breaking a sweat. If you're running a WooCommerce store, a membership site, or multiple WordPress installations, ScalaHosting's cloud VPS delivers noticeably faster page loads and more consistent performance during traffic spikes.

## FAQ

### Is InterServer's $2.50/mo price-lock genuine?

Yes. InterServer's price-lock guarantee means your renewal rate stays the same as your intro rate. As long as you keep the same plan, your price does not increase. This is one of the strongest pricing guarantees in the hosting industry — most hosts double or triple prices on renewal.

### Can I upgrade from InterServer shared to ScalaHosting later?

Yes. Both providers offer free unlimited migrations. Start with InterServer's shared plan while your site is small, then migrate to ScalaHosting's managed cloud VPS when you outgrow shared hosting resources. ScalaHosting's team handles the migration for you.

### Does ScalaHosting offer shared hosting?

ScalaHosting offers shared hosting starting at $3.95/mo, but its real strength is its managed cloud VPS plans. For basic shared hosting, InterServer is more cost-effective. ScalaHosting makes the most sense starting at the cloud VPS tier.

### Do I need managed VPS hosting for a single WordPress site?

Not if your site gets under 10,000 monthly visitors and doesn't run heavy plugins. InterServer's shared hosting handles that comfortably. Consider ScalaHosting's managed VPS when your traffic grows, you add ecommerce functionality, or you start running multiple sites.

### Which is better for reseller hosting?

InterServer has the edge on price with its reseller plans. ScalaHosting offers more flexibility with SPanel's multi-account management. If you're price-sensitive as a reseller, InterServer wins. If you want better client isolation and management tools, ScalaHosting is the better choice.
