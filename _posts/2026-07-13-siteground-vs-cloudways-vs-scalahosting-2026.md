## Why This Comparison Matters

SiteGround, Cloudways, and ScalaHosting represent three distinct tiers of managed WordPress hosting — and understanding the difference is the difference between overpaying and getting exactly what you need.

**SiteGround** is premium shared hosting on Google Cloud infrastructure. You share server resources with other accounts, but you get excellent support, WordPress-specific optimizations, and a smooth setup process. It's the closest thing to "it just works" in the hosting world.

**Cloudways** is a managed cloud platform. You get a dedicated VPS on your choice of provider (DigitalOcean, Vultr, AWS, Google Cloud), and Cloudways handles the server stack — Nginx, Varnish, Redis, PHP-FPM — through an abstraction layer. You get dedicated resources with managed convenience.

**ScalaHosting** is fully managed cloud VPS. You get a proper VPS with dedicated CPU cores, guaranteed RAM, and NVMe storage, plus SPanel — their own free control panel that replaces cPanel. They manage the operating system, security patches, and offer AI-powered malware protection.

All three call themselves "managed hosting," but they deliver it very differently. Here's how they stack up across the factors that actually matter.

<summary>Performance: How They Stack Up</summary>

Performance is where these three services diverge most — because they're fundamentally different architectures.

### SiteGround: Google Cloud Shared Infrastructure

SiteGround runs on Google Cloud Platform's premium tier network. Their stack combines NGINX as a reverse proxy, a custom dynamic cache, and Memcached for object caching. The GrowBig and GoGeek plans include "Ultrafast PHP" — a custom PHP handler that's roughly 30% faster than standard PHP-FPM.

Based on third-party benchmarks:
- **Cached TTFB:** ~180ms (US East)
- **LCP (Elementor page):** ~1.2s
- **GTmetrix:** 98/100 A grade

The caveat: you're sharing resources. SiteGround's "unlimited" traffic has a fair-use ceiling. A sustained spike from a viral post or aggressive ad campaign could get you throttled or asked to upgrade. For most small to medium WordPress sites, this isn't an issue — but it's worth knowing.

### Cloudways: Dedicated VPS with ThunderStack

Cloudways gives you a dedicated VPS with the ThunderStack — NGINX (static + reverse proxy), Varnish (full-page cache), Apache (htaccess compat), and Redis (object cache) all pre-configured and tuned. You get guaranteed resources: your RAM, your CPU, your NVMe storage.

Based on benchmarks:
- **Cached TTFB:** ~95ms (DO 2GB, US East)
- **LCP:** ~0.8s (same Elementor test page)
- **Load test (50 concurrent):** Zero 5xx errors, consistent sub-300ms response

The Varnish + Redis combination is the key advantage for WordPress. Varnish serves cached pages before PHP even runs, and Redis handles database query caching. For WooCommerce stores, membership sites, or any site with logged-in users, this caching stack makes a measurable difference.

### ScalaHosting: Dedicated VPS with SPanel + LiteSpeed

ScalaHosting gives you a dedicated cloud VPS with guaranteed resources — 2 dedicated CPU cores, 4 GB RAM, and NVMe storage on Build #1. The SPanel control panel runs on top of a LiteSpeed web server, which includes built-in caching and is known for handling PHP more efficiently than standard Apache.

The SShield AI security system runs in real time, blocking malware, brute force attacks, and known exploit patterns. According to ScalaHosting, it blocks an average of 300,000+ attacks per day across their network with a 99.9% block rate.

For database-driven WordPress sites (WooCommerce with 500+ products, membership sites with 10k+ users, LMS platforms), the dedicated CPU cores and NVMe I/O speeds make a real difference in admin panel responsiveness and page load times under load.

<summary>FAQ</summary>

### What's the main difference between SiteGround, Cloudways, and ScalaHosting?

SiteGround is premium shared hosting on Google Cloud infrastructure — you share resources but get excellent support and WordPress-specific optimizations. Cloudways is a managed cloud platform that gives you a dedicated VPS on your choice of provider (DO, Vultr, AWS, GCP) with a caching stack pre-configured. ScalaHosting is fully managed cloud VPS with dedicated resources, SPanel control panel, and AI-powered security. The core difference is resource allocation: shared vs dedicated VPS, and the level of control you get over the server.

### Which is cheapest in the first year?

SiteGround wins by a wide margin. The StartUp plan at $2.99/mo intro comes to $35.88 for year one. Cloudways at ~$14/mo comes to ~$168. ScalaHosting at $29.95/mo intro comes to $359.40. However, SiteGround's renewal price jumps to $17.99/mo after the first term.

### Which has the best support?

SiteGround has the best support experience for beginners — fast chat responses, real WordPress experts who don't read scripts. The support is widely considered the best in shared hosting. Cloudways and ScalaHosting both offer competent 24/7 support, but SiteGround's response times and quality of human interaction are noticeably better.

### Can I host multiple sites on each platform?

Yes, but the approach differs. SiteGround's GrowBig plan supports unlimited websites on a single account but you're still sharing server resources. Cloudways lets you host multiple WordPress installations on a single server and pay only for the resources you use. ScalaHosting's Build #1 supports unlimited websites with dedicated resources — you get the best of both worlds (unlimited sites + guaranteed resources) at a higher price point.

### Do I need technical skills for any of these?

SiteGround requires the least technical skill — their guided setup, AI assistant, and excellent support make it the best choice for absolute beginners. Cloudways has a moderate learning curve — you'll need to understand concepts like servers vs applications, Varnish cache, and vertical scaling. ScalaHosting's SPanel feels familiar to anyone who's used cPanel, and the managed VPS support handles the technical heavy lifting.

### Which one handles traffic spikes best?

Cloudways and ScalaHosting handle traffic spikes better than SiteGround because you have dedicated resources. On SiteGround, a sudden traffic surge from a viral post or ad campaign could trigger their fair-use policy and get your site throttled. On Cloudways, you can vertically scale your server (more RAM, more CPU) with a few clicks. On ScalaHosting, your dedicated resources absorb traffic spikes up to your plan's capacity.

### Is there a money-back guarantee?

SiteGround offers 30-day money-back. Cloudways offers a 3-day free trial (no credit card required) rather than a refund policy. ScalaHosting offers 30-day money-back on their managed VPS plans.

### Which has better security?

ScalaHosting's SShield AI is the most proactive security system of the three — it monitors for attacks in real time and blocks 99.9% before they reach your site. SiteGround's SG Site Scanner monitors for malware and known vulnerabilities. Cloudways offers IP whitelisting, bot protection, and Let's Encrypt SSL, but expects you to manage application-level security yourself.
