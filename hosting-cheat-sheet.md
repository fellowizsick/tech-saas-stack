## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Unlimited everything** (usually means throttled)  
❌ **No free SSL** (Let's Encrypt is free)  
❌ **No staging environment** (critical for safe updates)  
❌ **Forced annual contracts** (no monthly option)  
❌ **Hidden renewal pricing** (intro vs. renewal gap > 50%)  
❌ **No free migrations** (you'll pay $100-300)  
❌ **EIG-owned brands** (Bluehost, HostGator, iPage — often overloaded)

## Quick Questions to Ask Before Buying

1. **What's the REAL renewal price?** (Not intro price)
2. **Is staging included on ALL plans?**
3. **How many CPU cores / RAM guaranteed?** (Not "shared")
4. **What's the backup retention period?**
5. **Can I pay monthly?**
6. **Do you own your data centers or resell?**
7. **What's your uptime SLA?** (99.9% minimum)
8. **Free migrations — any limits?**
