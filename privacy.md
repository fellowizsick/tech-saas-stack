## 4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

| Cookie Type | Purpose | Duration | Control |
|-------------|---------|----------|---------|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` (GA4) | Anonymous analytics | 2 years | Browser settings / GA opt-out |
| Affiliate session cookies | Commission attribution | Session - 30 days | Browser settings |
| `localStorage` (newsletter) | Remember subscription | Until cleared | Clear site data |
| `localStorage` (theme preference) | Remember dark/light mode | Until cleared | Clear site data |

You can:
- **Opt out of Google Analytics**: [GA Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- **Disable cookies**: Browser settings → Privacy → Block third-party cookies
- **Clear localStorage**: Browser dev tools → Application → Local Storage → Delete
